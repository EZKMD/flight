# flight 0.1.0

V0.1.0 is the first stable baseline of the minimalist macOS/Linux terminal
flight tracker.

## Included

- AirLabs commercial occurrence and coherent physical-leg resolution
- exact ICAO24 joining to current OpenSky telemetry
- explicit `LIVE`, `TRACKING`, `STALE`, `SCHEDULED`, and `OFFLINE` states
- telemetry-derived climb, cruise, descent, and taxi phases
- provider-confirmed airborne and landed states
- live great-circle and schedule-fallback progress with provenance
- responsive wide, medium, compact, tiny, and insufficient-terminal layouts
- subtle animation, live resizing, keyboard refresh/quit, and terminal recovery
- deterministic offline fixtures and credential-safe provider diagnostics
- occurrence-specific metadata cache and broad static airport validation
- provider-neutral module boundaries and documented inactive extension points

## Validation

Manual observations covered cruising, climbing, descending, boarding, scheduled,
landed, active-without-telemetry, and the complete fresh-live path. See
`docs/v0.1-validation.md` for the matrix.

## Known limitations

OpenSky coverage can be absent or intermittent, especially on oceanic routes.
AirLabs may omit ICAO24 or aircraft metadata. Schedule progress is a time estimate,
not position or landing confirmation. Some designator formats are unsupported.
Airport data is a static generated snapshot. Historical telemetry, active
history collection, future visual modes, and airport-board mode are not included.

## Architecture

Provider-specific parsing is isolated behind resolver and telemetry contracts.
The normalizer produces the only `FlightState` consumed by the renderer. The
runtime independently schedules input, resize, animation, redraw, and provider
refresh. Archify diagrams are documentation artifacts and are not linked into
the binary.

## Future direction

Potential route-map and altitude-profile visuals, richer aircraft artwork,
historical playback, weather, and a separate multi-flight airport-board mode are
documented without being exposed as V0.1 features.
